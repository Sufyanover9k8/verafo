Questions sellers actually ask

Straight answers

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. Nothing is charged until the trial ends.

What happens after my Founder One trial ends?

You move to PKR 12,499 a month and stay there permanently, even when list price rises.

Can I switch plans?

Yes, any time from inside the app. Shopify prorates the difference.

What if Verafo does not save me a single return?

The guarantee fires automatically. No form to fill in.

Who owns my customer data?

You do, always. Your customer list is never shared with another merchant.

Can another seller see my customers?

No. Only a derived score crosses between stores, the way a credit score works. Details in our privacy policy.

Are you reading my customers' WhatsApp messages?

No. Only structured metadata, such as whether a reply arrived.

What about a brand new buyer?

The score falls back to address history, order timing and geography until about three orders accumulate.
