1.3 What other sellers on the network can and cannot see

Verafo operates a shared network in which a buyer's behavioural history, expressed only as a Derived Signal such as a risk score, may be made visible to a different seller when that same buyer places an order with them. This is the mechanism by which the Service predicts buyer reliability across multiple sellers.

When this occurs:

  • Another seller will only ever receive a Derived Signal (for example, a risk classification such as "low risk", "medium risk", or "high risk") in relation to a buyer they are themselves transacting with.
  • Another seller will never receive your raw Customer Data, including the buyer's name, full order history, or any other identifying detail belonging to your business relationship with that buyer.
  • No seller can search, browse, export, or otherwise access another seller's Customer Data through the Service.

1.4 Corrected summary

To state this plainly: Verafo's infrastructure processes your Customer Data in order to operate the Service for you, and that processing is necessary and unavoidable. What Verafo does not do is expose your raw Customer Data to any third party, including other sellers on the platform. Only anonymised, derived risk signals are shared across the network, never the underlying records.
